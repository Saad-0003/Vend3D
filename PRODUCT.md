# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creator-led brands and direct-to-consumer operators who want to launch and test physical product drops.

## Product Purpose

Vend3D lets teams customize physical products in a 3D studio, preview the result, and order from a single unit.

## Positioning

The product combines live 3D customization and product proofing with single-piece ordering.

## Operating Context

Users browse a product catalog, adjust product appearance in the studio, and prepare limited drops without a minimum run.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product appearance can be customized and previewed in the browser.
- Orders can start at one unit.
- Payments are demo-only; there is no live payment gateway.
- Review copy supplied for the `/reviews` page is demo placeholder content, not verified customer evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a configured sample catalog and the review copy supplied for the demo. No verified review evidence is available.

## Product Principles

- Make product customization previewable before ordering.
- Support small experiments without requiring a large production run.
- Keep illustrative content distinct from verified product or customer evidence.
